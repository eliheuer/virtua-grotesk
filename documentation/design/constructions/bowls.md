# Bowls

A lowercase bowl (b d p q g) is the round half of the `o`, copied point for
point, joined to a stem. The round curve, its wall and its vertical extent
come from the `o`. The counter width is the only value set by eye.

These rules come from Eli's hand-drawn Regular `d` (commit `88b4aa2`) and his
correction of an agent's `g`. The numbers are Regular values at the time of
that work. Measure the current green `o` before you use them.

## Rules

1. **Copy the o's round half.** Copy the outer and inner arcs of the `o` on
   the side of the bowl. Do not shift, scale or re-snap an older bowl into
   place. Regular `o` at the time: outer extremum x 40, inner extremum x 138,
   round wall 98, inner extent y 68 to 508, outer extent y -16 to 592.
2. **Advance and sidebearings follow the o.** The advance is the `o` advance
   (624 in Regular). The round side keeps the `o` sidebearing (40); the stem
   side gets 64.
3. **The counter is about 0.95 of the o counter** (about 330 against 348).
   The closed stem side looks heavier than the open `o`.
4. **Draw the bowl-to-stem junction.** Its handles move in x and y. Tune it
   for an even tangent as the bowl leaves the stem.
5. **Keep the inner bulge.** The inner extremum of the bowl passes the inner
   edge of the stem by about 4 units. That is roundness. Do not flatten it.
6. **Symmetry is optical.** The top and bottom of a bowl can differ by a few
   units.

## b, p and q from d

When one bowl letter is approved, derive the others from it in each master:

- `b` is `d` mirrored horizontally. `q` is `d` mirrored vertically. `p` is
  `d` rotated 180 degrees.
- A mirror reverses the contour direction, so reverse each contour after a
  mirror. A rotation keeps the direction.
- After a vertical mirror, move the descender terminal so that its bottom is
  on the descender line.

## Single-storey g

The `g` is not a mirror of `d`. Its bowl is shorter and higher than the `o`
(Regular inner extent y 108 to 500) to make space for the ear and the tail.

- The inner counter follows the bowl rules, with its extrema on the center
  of the bowl.
- The outer round side is slightly flatter than the `o` (vertical handles
  ±160 about the bowl center, not ±166).
- The top left is fuller than the bottom left, where the bowl meets the tail.
  Do not mirror the bowl top to bottom.
- The ear, spine and tail are drawn by eye. Eli set the tail depth to -216.

## Errors to avoid

- A bowl shifted until its counter matches. The numbers pass, but the curve
  is not the `o`.
- An advance set for the group (for example 636) instead of the `o` advance.
- A wall flattened to a constant thickness.
