# Turns, junctions and landings

Identify each connection as a smooth tangent continuation, straight landing,
deliberate cut, shelf, or intended inflection. Choose straight-run boundaries and
turn extents explicitly before fitting handles. Inspect marked and unmarked joins.

Keep paired contour edges in view: improving one edge's curvature can make the
stroke uneven. Whole-silhouette articulation and apparent mass govern the repair.
Do not remove Seen's lobes to obtain a smooth band or stretch a turn to an arbitrary
intersection. Do not impose equal handles on all curved marks.

The Zayin, Gimel, Final Tsadi and hamza corrections are examples for one glyph
and master each. They are not general formulas.
