Virtua Grotesk is a geometric sans serif designed by Eli Heuer. Its construction uses a 1024-unit em, a power-of-two grid, and 16-unit chamfers at sharp corners. The upright family has a weight axis from Regular (400) to Bold (700), with Medium and SemiBold instances. To contribute, see github.com/eliheuer/virtua-grotesk.

The design combines straight stems and beveled junctions with smooth curved bowls. Weight changes generally reduce the counters while retaining the outer proportions. The source files record the outlines, spacing, kerning, anchors, and OpenType features in two UFO masters and a designspace.

Latin, Arabic, and Hebrew are planned for the first release. The font is still in development: encoded characters and generated outlines require drawing, shaping, and language review before their presence can be treated as complete script support.

AI tools have assisted with code, documentation, and candidate glyph generation. Generated candidates are marked for human review in the source files. The font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
