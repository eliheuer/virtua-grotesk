Virtua Grotesk is a geometric grotesk with a weight axis from Regular to Bold. Its defining construction detail is a system of 16-unit chamfered corners, giving sharp junctions a consistent 45-degree bevel while preserving a monolinear texture.

The family supports Latin and Arabic, and is designed for interfaces, editorial settings, and display typography where a technical grotesk voice is useful. Its weight range runs from Regular to Bold, with intermediate styles for typographic hierarchy.
