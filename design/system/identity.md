# Virtua construction

Virtua is a geometric grotesk on a dyadic grid with straight chamfered corners,
monolinear apparent weight, deliberate smooth cubic curves and open counters.
The intended character is clean, neo-modernist and machined. Preserve the
established skeleton and articulation; a smoother curve is not automatically a
better Virtua drawing.

Read live green Virtua source geometry as well as filled renders. Identify the
exact donor glyph, master and contour run. External fonts guide script structure
and reading texture; they do not replace Virtua construction or named intent.

Plan silhouette, straight runs, turns, landings and cuts before fitting handles.
Inspect the whole filled drawing at large and reading sizes, then use diagnostics
to investigate faults. Numerical improvement never establishes human approval.

Measured Zayin, Gimel, Final Tsadi and hamza corrections are scoped examples.
Their radii, handles and shelves are not universal formulas. Consult the relevant
correction and its counterexamples when transferring construction.
