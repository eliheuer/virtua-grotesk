# Batch 27 — Bold medial Doachashmee

One active source changes: Bold `hehDoachashmee-ar.medi`, red to purple, awaiting
human review. It has no component consumers. All Regular sources, including the
RGBA-only green medial, the other three Doachashmee forms, all 18 recursive Beh
reservations, and manual Bold hamza remain byte-identical. `inspection.json`
records live colors, consumers and reserved hashes; `applied.json` holds the full
source guard and exact before/candidate snapshots.

## Structure before curves

The baseline four-form raster exposes a major source mismatch: isolated and
final Doachashmee are single Goal-like bowls. Noto Sans Arabic and IBM Plex Sans
Arabic show two-eye construction; their initial form also differs from our
vertical two-loop initial. These three forms need a separate paired-master
structural repair. This batch does not relabel them as satisfactory or apply a
polished Goal bowl to them. Regular isolated/final/initial are orange; preserve
their existing design while preparing the concrete structural proposal.

The medial form has the appropriate upper/lower two-loop construction. The old
Bold curves had angled extrema handles, distorted counters, a reversing shoulder
and collapsed joining landings. This bounded repair preserves that construction
and its original 12/12/32-point topology while correcting those faults.

## Drawing

Use the family-native medial Heh construction measured in batch 25, with the
same 160-unit loop extremum weights and 176-unit joining band. This is an AI
working construction, not a green design reference. No external font outline is
copied. Keep Doachashmee's own anchors and smooth flags. Its green Regular
outline differs from Regular Arabic Heh; do not replace it by that donor.

Bold advance 704; top 496, lower loop bottom -352. Upper loop outer x64..592,
inner x224..432; lower outer right624, inner right464. The counter curves have
axis-aligned extrema handles and open apertures. Outer joining terminals retain
16-unit cuts; short internal landings/cuts remain 8 units. The increased width
and depth are working proportions pending human review. All new geometry is on8.
