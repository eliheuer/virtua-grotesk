## Drawing contract

Preserve the established initial sloping head and medial double-loop skeletons,
with the original compatible 37/12 and 12/12/32 point contours. Replace skewed
extremum handles, unintended curvature reversals and pinched counters with
axis-tangent turns. All new coordinates and advances use the 8-unit lattice.
Both forms join through a 176-unit bar. Initial loop side/top weights are 160;
its counter is x224..416, y176..336. The open space under the diagonal remains
open, as in the protected Regular construction. Do not mistake it for a second
closed contour. The initial grows to advance992 and y736 to accommodate these
weights; its increased size and open diagonal-to-loop relationship need human
review against the reference constructions, not automatic acceptance.

Medial has a 160-unit left stem and outer top/bottom weights, with upper counter
x224..432 and lower x224..464. Bounds y-352..496, advance704. Keep intentional
8-unit inner landings at the bar junctions. Existing anchor coordinates stay
unchanged for the attachment pass; they no longer track the enlarged loops.

Large-detail inspection prompted another local handle adjustment on the initial
inner turn and medial outer shoulder. A trial handle change introduced an
inflection; the signed profile caught it and the rejected coordinates were
revised before the final evidence was compiled. Smooth flags alone and a
passing tangent-angle test would not have caught this. The final profiles retain
unequal signed curvature at several G1 joins rather than claiming all-G2 curves.
